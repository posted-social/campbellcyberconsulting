FAQ

Palo Alto firewall questions, answered.

Are you affiliated with Palo Alto Networks?

No. Campbell Cyber Consulting is an independent consultancy that specializes in Palo Alto Networks firewalls. We aren’t affiliated with or endorsed by Palo Alto Networks, so our recommendations are based on what your environment needs, not on a sales quota.

Do you work with MSPs on a white-label basis?

Yes. Managed service providers can bring us in for one-off projects, overflow capacity, or as a white-label Palo Alto specialist they resell to their own clients at a markup. We work under your brand and your process, and the client relationship stays yours.

What does a Palo Alto firewall health check include?

A health check reviews your PAN-OS version and upgrade path, security policy and rule hygiene, threat prevention and URL filtering profiles, decryption, high availability, GlobalProtect, logging, and administrative access. You get a written report that ranks findings by risk, with clear next steps.

Which firewalls can you migrate to Palo Alto?

We migrate from common third-party platforms, including Cisco ASA and Firepower, Fortinet FortiGate, SonicWall, and WatchGuard. Each migration includes a rule review, conversion, a tested cutover plan, and a rollback plan.

How much does Palo Alto firewall consulting cost?

Health checks are a fixed price per firewall or HA pair. Migrations are quoted as a fixed project price after a short discovery call. Retainers are a monthly block of hours. See the pricing section for starting prices.

Do you work on-site or remotely?

Both. We’re based in Glen Carbon, Illinois, and work on-site across the St. Louis metro and Metro East. Most health checks and retainer work happen remotely over a secure session.
